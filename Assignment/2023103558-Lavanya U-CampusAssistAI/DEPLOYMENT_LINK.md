## Description

CampusAssist AI is a lightweight Agentic AI web application that helps students analyze campus-related issues and receive AI-assisted recommendations.

The application uses two logical AI agents:

1. **Triage Agent** – analyzes the student's issue and determines its category, priority, reasoning and confidence.
2. **Action Agent** – recommends the appropriate campus department, next action and escalation status.
