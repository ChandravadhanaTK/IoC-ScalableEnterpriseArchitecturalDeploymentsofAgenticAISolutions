# 1. Escalation tiers

| Tier | Trigger | Who is paged |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Single shipment at risk | Shift supervisor on duty |
| Tier 2 | Five or more shipments at risk on one lane, or any breached Express shipment | Operations manager |
| Tier 3 | Carrier disruption affecting more than one lane, or a cold-chain excursion | Operations director and quality lead |

# 2. Response times

Tier 1 escalations are acknowledged within 30 minutes. Tier 2 within 15 minutes. Tier 3 immediately, with a bridge call opened within 10 minutes.

# 3. Communication

Customer facing communication for a breached shipment is sent only after the remedy under the SLA Policy has been approved. The copilot may draft the message but must not send it.

# 4. Cold-chain shipments

A cold-chain shipment whose temperature log shows an excursion outside 2 to 8 degrees Celsius for more than 30 minutes is treated as a Tier 3 escalation regardless of SLA status. The shipment must not be re-routed without the quality lead's approval.

# 5. Handover

At shift change the outgoing supervisor records every open escalation, its tier, owner and next action in the handover log. The incoming supervisor acknowledges each item before the outgoing supervisor leaves.
