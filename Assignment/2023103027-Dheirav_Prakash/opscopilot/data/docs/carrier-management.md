# 1. Carrier onboarding

A carrier is onboarded after a documented capability check covering fleet size, lane coverage, insurance certificates and tracking API conformance. Each carrier is assigned a carrier code and one or more lanes.

# 2. Performance review

A carrier enters performance review when its breach rate exceeds 8 percent over a rolling 30 day window on any lane, or when it declares more than two service disruptions in a calendar month. While on performance review the carrier is excluded from automatic re-route selection and from new lane assignments.

# 3. Exit from review

The carrier exits performance review after 30 consecutive days with a breach rate below 5 percent on the affected lane, confirmed by network planning.

# 4. Disruption declarations

Carriers must declare a service disruption through the carrier portal within two hours of becoming aware of it. The declaration names the lane, the expected duration and the shipments affected. The operations copilot treats an open declaration as grounds for re-routing under SOP-OPS-014.
