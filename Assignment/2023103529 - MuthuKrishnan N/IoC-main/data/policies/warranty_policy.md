# Enterprise Limited Warranty Policy

**Policy Document ID:** POL-WAR-2026-v1  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applicability:** Electronic hardware and peripheral devices

---

## 1. Standard Warranty Coverage
- All hardware products carry a standard **12-month manufacturer limited warranty** from the date of confirmed delivery.
- The warranty covers manufacturing defects, hardware failure under normal operating conditions, and internal component faults.

---

## 2. Exclusions from Warranty
- Physical damage caused by drops, spills, improper electrical surge, unauthorized modifications, or repair attempts by uncertified parties.
- Consumable parts (e.g. batteries, cables) subjected to natural wear and tear after 90 days.

---

## 3. Warranty Service Process
- If an item malfunctions after the 7-day replacement window but within 12 months, the AI Agent creates an authorized Warranty Repair Ticket (`TCK-WAR-xxxx`).
- High-value hardware replacements under warranty require verification of serial number and human technical agent sign-off.
