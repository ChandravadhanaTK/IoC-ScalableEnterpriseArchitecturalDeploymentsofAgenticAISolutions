# Enterprise Replacement Policy

**Policy Document ID:** POL-REP-2026-v2  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applicability:** All physical product orders

---

## 1. Replacement Eligibility Window
A customer may request a replacement within **7 days of delivery** under the following verified conditions:
- **Damaged on Arrival:** The product packaging or hardware arrived physically damaged during transit.
- **Wrong Item Delivered:** The item received does not match the product listed on the order invoice (e.g., incorrect model, color, or specifications).
- **Dead on Arrival (DOA):** The product fails to power on or perform its primary intended function upon unboxing.

---

## 2. Automated vs. Supervised Replacement Rules
- **Automated Replacement Criteria:**
  1. The complaint is filed within **7 days** of confirmed package delivery date.
  2. The item unit value is **under ₹10,000**.
  3. The customer has a verified account in good standing.
  4. The product is in-stock at the nearest fulfillment fulfillment node.
  If all criteria are satisfied, the AI Resolution Agent can dispatch an automated replacement order immediately (`REP-xxxxx`).

- **Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop Review:**
  1. Orders with item values exceeding **₹10,000** require human support agent sign-off.
  2. Claims submitted after the 7-day delivery threshold must be redirected to warranty processing or human discretion.
  3. Cases where the initial order status shows "In Transit" or "Pending Delivery" cannot receive replacement without human verification.

---

## 3. Return of Damaged Unit
- For high-value goods (> ₹3,000), a reverse pickup courier will be assigned simultaneously with the replacement order generation.
- The customer must hand over the damaged unit in its original packaging when the replacement is delivered.
