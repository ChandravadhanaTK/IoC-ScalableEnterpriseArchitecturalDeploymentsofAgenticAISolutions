# Enterprise Refund Policy

**Policy Document ID:** POL-REF-2026-v2  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applicability:** All retail and enterprise customer purchases

---

## 1. General Refund Eligibility
Customers may request a refund for eligible purchases subject to the terms and timelines outlined in this policy:
- **Refund Window:** Refund requests must be initiated within **14 calendar days** of order delivery.
- **Item Condition:** Returned items must be in original condition, including original packaging, accessories, manuals, and proof of purchase (Order ID).
- **Non-Refundable Categories:** Digital downloads, customized enterprise goods, perishable items, and software licenses that have been activated cannot be refunded.

---

## 2. Refund Processing and Financial Limits
- **Automated Processing Limit:** Standard refunds with an order value **less than or equal to ₹5,000 (INR 5,000)** are eligible for automatic resolution by the AI Resolution Agent, provided the order status confirms successful return receipt or verified cancellation prior to dispatch.
- **High-Value Threshold & Mandatory Human Approval:** Any refund request where the requested amount exceeds **₹5,000 (INR 5,000)** requires mandatory human review and approval by an authorized Support Agent. Automated refund execution for amounts > ₹5,000 is strictly prohibited by enterprise compliance guardrails.
- **Payment Method:** Approved refunds will be credited back to the original method of payment within **5-7 business days**.

---

## 3. Damaged or Defective Items
- If an item arrives damaged or defective, the customer is entitled to either an immediate free replacement or a full refund.
- For damaged items, photographic proof of damage is required if requested by the support agent or automated verification tool.
- If damage is reported after 14 days but within the warranty period, warranty repair or replacement procedures apply instead of standard refunds.

---

## 4. Exceptions and Escalations
- **Suspected Fraud or Abuse:** Accounts flagged for high return velocity or anomalous refund requests will have automated actions suspended and will be escalated to Senior Support Review.
- **Policy Overrides:** Only Tier-2 Support Managers or Administrators hold the authority to approve refunds outside the standard 14-day window.
