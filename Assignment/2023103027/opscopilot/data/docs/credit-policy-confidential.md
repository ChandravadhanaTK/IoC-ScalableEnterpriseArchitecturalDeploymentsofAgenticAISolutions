# 1. Scope

This policy governs the issue of goodwill and SLA credits to customer accounts. It is confidential to the finance and operations management groups. Team leads and shift supervisors do not have access to this document.

# 2. Authorisation limits

| Role | Maximum single credit |
|---|---|
| Team lead | 2,000 INR |
| Shift supervisor | 5,000 INR |
| Operations manager | 25,000 INR |
| Finance controller | 100,000 INR |

# 3. Monthly caps

Credits to a single customer account are capped at 50,000 INR per calendar month without finance controller approval. The copilot must check the running monthly total before proposing a credit.

# 4. Reserve accounting

Approved credits are booked against the operations goodwill reserve, cost centre 4410. Credits that arise from a carrier caused breach are recovered from the carrier under the penalty clause of the carrier contract.

# 5. Audit

Every credit records the approving identity, the shipment id, the SLA breach reference and the policy version in force. Records are retained for seven years.
